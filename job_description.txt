Associate Applied AI Engineer
Northstar Labs · Pune, India · Hybrid · Full-time

About the role
Northstar Labs builds software that turns business documents and text into reliable, structured data. We are looking for an Associate Applied AI Engineer to help prototype and improve language-model features used in internal and customer-facing workflows.

What you will do
- Build Python services that process documents such as PDF and Word files.
- Use hosted language-model APIs to extract, classify, and summarize information.
- Validate model responses with typed schemas and return clean JSON through APIs.
- Create evaluation examples, review errors, and improve prompts and extraction quality.
- Work with product and engineering teammates on testing, documentation, and safe handling of confidential data.

What we are looking for
- Two or more years of experience with Python software development, data engineering, or applied machine learning.
- Experience with REST APIs, SQL, Git, and automated testing.
- Familiarity with an LLM API, NLP, information extraction, or document-processing tools.
- Experience with Pydantic or another data-validation library is helpful.
- Clear communication and a practical approach to debugging.
- A degree in computer science, engineering, statistics, or equivalent practical experience.

Experience deploying services with Docker or a cloud platform is a plus.
